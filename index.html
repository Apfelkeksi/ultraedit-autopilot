UltraEdit Autopilot

UltraEdit Autopilot is a private tool that the owner of the YouTube channels "Would You Rather Duels" and its sister map channel runs on his own computer. It renders short videos and schedules them for upload to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.

The tool has exactly one user: the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other person's account or data.
